About me

From the bench
to the terminal.

Valencia, Spain, Europe

I am a bioinformatician based in Valencia with a background in biotechnology. I started at the bench, editing wine yeasts with CRISPR-Cas9 and running aging assays in C. elegans, and moved to computational biology to work with the data those experiments produce.

Today I develop long-read transcriptomics tools in the SQANTI ecosystem at Ana Conesa’s Genomics of Gene Expression Lab (I2SysBio, UV-CSIC), and I can take on any kind of bioinformatic analysis, with long or short reads, from raw data to results.
