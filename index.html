03

Open-source software · Structural bioinformatics

3D Protein Simulator

Pseudo-molecular dynamics from experimentally solved structures. From a single PDB code, it finds other deposited structures of the same protein with BLAST, superimposes them in PyMOL and renders an animation in which the protein moves between its real conformations: domain closure, hinge motions or the rearrangement that follows ligand binding.

  • Python
  • Biopython
  • BLAST
  • PyMOL
PDB → GIF one command
